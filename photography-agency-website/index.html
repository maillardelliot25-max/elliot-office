About the Agency

Full production, no outsourcing

Liming Lens Productions is a Trinidad-grown crew built for live events — from a single artist shoot to full multi-camera festival coverage. Everything is shot, edited and delivered locally.

Full-show stage photography from load-in to encore — wide stage shots, lighting cues and performance moments.

Press-ready portraits, promo shots and press kit imagery for performers, bands and DJs.

Full-day coverage of festivals, fetes and Carnival events — crowd energy, mas bands and stage acts.

Aerial sweeps of stages, festival grounds and crowds for scale and scope no ground camera can capture.

Multiple angles shot and switched live for broadcast-ready recap videos and livestreams.

Product launches, activations and brand events shot for social content and ad campaigns.

Short-form, high-energy content built for maximum reach, plus gear and crew support for influencer collabs and sponsor activations.

    Careers & Training

    The 12-Month Contract & Training Program

    We're building the next generation of Trini photographers and filmmakers. No experience? No problem — we train you from the ground up, then put you to work.

    Months 1–3

    Probation & Free Training

    • Free professional courses (stage photography, drone piloting, multi-cam switching)
    • Hands-on shadowing at real live shows
    • Mentorship from the core crew
    • No cost to you — training is fully covered
    Months 4–9

    Working Contract

    • Paid production work on real live events
    • Progressive responsibility & skill sign-off
    • Continued coaching & portfolio building
    Months 10–12

    Specialization & Review

    • Choose a lane: stage photography, drone, multi-cam video or artist portraits
    • Lead-shooter opportunities on select shows
    • Performance review for long-term contract renewal
