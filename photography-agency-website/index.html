Careers & Training

The 12-Month Contract & Training Program

We're building the next generation of Trini photographers and filmmakers. No experience? No problem — we train you from the ground up, then put you to work.

Months 1–3

Probation & Free Training

  • Free professional courses (camera, lighting, editing)
  • Hands-on shadowing on real shoots
  • Mentorship from the core crew
  • No cost to you — training is fully covered
Months 4–9

Working Contract

  • Paid production work on real client shoots
  • Progressive responsibility & skill sign-off
  • Continued coaching & portfolio building
Months 10–12

Specialization & Review

  • Choose a lane: photo, video, editing or drone
  • Lead-shooter opportunities on select jobs
  • Performance review for long-term contract renewal

The probation period is focused on free training, courses and hands-on shadowing rather than a salaried wage. From Month 4 onward, you move onto the working contract and are paid for production work.
