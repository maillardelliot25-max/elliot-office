# Character model credits

All three models (stored as self-contained glTF JSON) come from the three.js examples (github.com/mrdoob/three.js, `examples/models/gltf`). Textures were downscaled and unused animations removed for the web.

- `soldier.json`: "Vanguard" soldier from Mixamo (Adobe). Its Idle, Walk and Run animations drive every character.
- `michelle.json`: "Michelle" from Mixamo (Adobe).
- `male.json`: Ready Player Me avatar (`readyplayer.me.glb` in the three.js examples).

Check each provider's license terms before any public or commercial release.
