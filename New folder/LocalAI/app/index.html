Friday Lens: ask about your screen

    
Starts with the app. Flick the mouse (or your finger) up from the very bottom edge of the screen, or press the shortcut, and Friday looks at your screen. Restart the app after changing the swipe or shortcut.

    
    
    
    
    
    
    

      
      
    

    
Checks only when the screen changes, and offers help at most once every 10 minutes. "Auto" uses an offline model so your screen never leaves this PC; a ☁ model would send a screenshot every check.

    
🎞 Screen memory

    
Friday Lens saves a screenshot to this PC whenever your screen changes, grouped in 8-hour blocks (00-08, 08-16, 16-24). Old blocks are deleted automatically. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Takes effect within a minute; Lens must be running.

    
    

      
      
    

    

    
📱 Friday on your phone (Samsung / Android)

    
Install the Friday Android app (README, step 9), set it as your phone's digital assistant, then swipe up from a bottom corner in any app to ask about your screen. Answers come from this PC, so your phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi.
