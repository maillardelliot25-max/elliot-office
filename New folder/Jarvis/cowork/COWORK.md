# Jarvis + Claude Cowork

**Claude Cowork** (in the Claude desktop app) runs on your PC and does real work in your folders, asking
before anything risky. Jarvis gives it his memory, screen history, your details, your phone and his voice.
Together, Cowork is the worker and Jarvis is the home base.

## One-time setup (5 minutes)

1. Install the **Claude desktop app** (claude.ai/download) and sign in.
2. Add the two Jarvis skills: Claude → **Settings → Capabilities → Skills → Upload skill**, once for each:
   - `jarvis-setup.zip` teaches Cowork to install, update, start and repair Jarvis
   - `jarvis-assistant.zip` teaches Cowork to use Jarvis's memory, screen history, activity, PDFs and notifications
   (Download them from this folder on GitHub, or from the Jarvis folder once you have it.)
3. In Cowork, say: **"Set up Jarvis on this PC."** It downloads Jarvis, installs it, starts it, and tells you
   the address and PIN for your phone. It asks before each install.
4. When it offers the **Claude link**, say yes, then fully quit and reopen Claude. Now Cowork has Jarvis's tools.

## Things to say to Cowork

- "Update Jarvis." Gets the newest version; your memory, settings and paired devices are kept.
- "Jarvis isn't working, fix it."
- "Where did my time go this week? Make me a chart."
- "Find the flight price I looked at yesterday and put it in my travel notes."
- "Fill in this job application PDF with my details and sign it, then tell me on my phone."
- "Turn the receipts in Downloads into a spreadsheet, and let me know when it's done."
- "What did I tell Jarvis about my tablet?"

Cowork asks before running commands or changing files outside the folder you're working in. Jarvis's
own switches (app → PC → Switches) still decide what Jarvis records and shares.
